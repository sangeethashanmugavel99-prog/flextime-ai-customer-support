# FlexTime End-to-End Customer Support Example

## Customer Input

Someone logged into my FlexTime account and changed my settings. I don't recognize this activity and I'm worried my account has been compromised.

## Stage 1 — Intent Classification

Intent: Urgent Escalation

Urgency: High

Reason: The customer reports unrecognized account activity and possible account compromise.

## Stage 2 — CARE Response

I understand why you’re concerned about unrecognized activity on your FlexTime account. Since your account may have been accessed without your authorization, this needs to be reviewed by a human support team. Please contact FlexTime support for immediate assistance with the account security issue.

## Stage 3 — Self-Verification

Verification Result: PASS

The response was checked for intent accuracy, CARE compliance, empathy, factual accuracy, policy compliance, actionability, escalation awareness, and customer safety.

## Stage 4 — Escalation

Escalation Required: YES

Priority: CRITICAL

Recommended Team: Security

Reason: The customer reports unauthorized account access, unrecognized settings changes, and possible account compromise.

Customer Impact: The customer's account security may be at risk.

Required Human Action: Security team should review the reported unauthorized access and account changes and determine the appropriate security response.

## Stage 5 — CRM Summary

Customer Intent: Urgent Escalation

Customer Issue: Unrecognized account login and settings changes; possible account compromise.

Urgency: Critical

Customer Sentiment: Concerned

Actions Taken: Issue acknowledged and human security review recommended.

Resolution Status: Pending

Escalation Status: Escalation required

Assigned Team: Security

Next Action: Security team should review the reported unauthorized access and account changes and determine the appropriate security response.

## Final Outcome

The customer issue was classified correctly, an empathetic response was generated and verified, the security risk was escalated to the appropriate human team, and the conversation was converted into a CRM-ready summary.
