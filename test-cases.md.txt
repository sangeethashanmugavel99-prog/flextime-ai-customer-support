## Failure Case Test

### Scenario

Customer reported a duplicate subscription charge.

The AI-generated response incorrectly stated that the refund would be issued immediately.

### Verification Result

FAIL

### Detected Issues

* Unsupported refund promise.
* The AI did not have authority to guarantee a refund.
* The duplicate charge had not been independently confirmed.

### Required Correction

The response must state that the billing issue requires review by the appropriate billing team and must not guarantee a refund or compensation.

### Verification Outcome

The self-verification module correctly identified the unsupported claim and required correction.
